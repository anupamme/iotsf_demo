\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
% ============================================================

We presented \ours{}, a two-stage framework that leverages generative AI
to both stress-test and improve IoT intrusion detection systems.  By using
Diffusion-TS to generate protocol-compliant hard-negative attacks and
fine-tuning Moirai with a supervised contrastive objective, \ours{}
achieves significantly better detection on AI-generated stealthy attacks
than traditional and deep-learning baselines, particularly at the highest
stealth levels (95\%) where conventional methods fail.

Our key insight is that the most effective defence against AI-powered
adversaries is not a static model trained on historical attacks, but an
adaptive training regime that continuously exposes the detector to the
hardest possible synthetic attacks.  This \emph{red-team-as-training-data}
paradigm generalises beyond IoT security and may inform future work on
adversarial robustness in time-series anomaly detection broadly.

We release all code, synthetic datasets, calibration scripts, and an
interactive Streamlit demonstration to facilitate reproducibility and
encourage follow-up work in this critical security domain.
