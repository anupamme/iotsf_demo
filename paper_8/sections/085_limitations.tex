\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}
% ============================================================

Full detail is in Appendix~\ref{app:limitations}; broader impact is in the Ethics
Statement.
(1)~\emph{A null result is not a proof of absence, and the screen is a low bar.}
We report that no cell in our matrix meets the degradation definition, not that
degradation does not occur.  What would settle it is a cell with a \emph{large}
pre-trained margin over a \emph{strong} baseline, and that is the combination our
benchmarks do not supply: 27 of 32 cells lose to a fitted lookback-96 ridge map,
the five that beat it are \emph{all Moirai on ETTh2}, and no cell in the matrix
beats every admissible rung of the eight-baseline ladder
(Appendix~\ref{app:baselines}).  So the part of our result that rests on a
demonstrated pre-trained advantage is a claim about one series measured against
one weak baseline, and the five-survivor agreement in
\S\ref{sec:analysis:degradation} could reflect something about ETTh2 rather than
about fine-tuning.
(2)~\emph{Condition~D is a control on encoder weight updates, not on
representations.} It leaves the input projection and mask encoding trainable,
so its CKA is $0.969$--$0.9999$ on Moirai and as low as $0.38$ on TimesFM.
Condition~H (strict freeze, CKA exactly $1.0000$) closes that gap on 16 of the
31 cells; it confirms all five survivors but \emph{reverses} $\denc$'s sign on
all four ETTm2 cells, and 15 rest on D alone (Appendix~\ref{app:strictfreeze}).
(3)~\emph{Seed counts are small.} Cells carry 3 to 10 seeds, and the unanimity
requirement in clauses~(ii) and~(iii) is doing real work at those counts: the
closest candidate is excluded on a 5/10 split.
(4)~\emph{Horizon confounds the cross-backbone comparison.} The non-Moirai arm
runs at $h{=}24$ and the Moirai arm at $h{\in}\{96,192\}$, on different
normalisations; we report them separately and never pool them.
(5)~\emph{A retracted axis.} The linear-probe $\Delta R^2$ diagnostic is
withdrawn (Appendix~\ref{sec:retraction}), so we offer no account of \emph{what} the
encoder restructured---only whether the restructuring was necessary, which the
frozen-encoder comparison answers.
(6)~\emph{Similarity is not mechanism.} A representation can drift far and still
carry the task information in a transformed basis, and a small drift can destroy
one critical direction; our negative claim is about \emph{identifying} damage
from similarity alone, not about CKA's validity as a measure of similarity.
(7)~\emph{Also unsettled:} the forgetting sign at $n{=}10$k is
protocol-dependent; the ILI cell is small (${\approx}450$ windows); and two
pre-registered predictions failed (\S\ref{sec:analysis:prospective},
\S\ref{sec:analysis:ili}).
