\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}
% ============================================================

Full detail is in Appendix~\ref{app:limitations}; broader impact is in the Ethics Statement.
(1)~\emph{A null result is not a proof of absence, and the screen is a low bar.}
We report that no cell in our matrix meets the degradation definition, not that
degradation does not occur.  What would settle it is a cell with a \emph{large}
pre-trained margin over a \emph{strong} baseline, and that is the combination these
benchmarks do not supply (\S\ref{sec:forecasting:gate},
Appendix~\ref{app:baselines}).  The drift analyses run on the 20-cell union
spanning all three backbones, but the seven-survivor agreement in
\S\ref{sec:analysis:degradation} rests on one backbone and two series
(Appendix~\ref{app:limitations}).

(3)~\emph{Seed counts are small:} 3 to 10 per cell, so most per-cell calls are
inconclusive by construction, and two of the seven survivors would need 182 and
212 paired seeds to resolve their observed effect
(\S\ref{sec:analysis:degradation}).
(4)~\emph{Horizon confounds the cross-backbone comparison.} The non-Moirai arm
runs at $h{=}24$ and the Moirai arm at $h{\in}\{96,192\}$, on different
normalisations; we report them separately and never pool them.
