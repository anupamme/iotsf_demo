\label{sec:exp2}
% ============================================================

\textbf{What the treatment is.}  Conditions B and D differ in exactly one
thing (whether the encoder stack's weights may move), so $\denc$
(Equation~\ref{eq:denc}) estimates the contribution of encoder weight updates and
not of representational change.  Where that boundary is drawn is load-bearing:
D leaves the input projection and mask encoding trainable, and the strict freeze
that closes that gap on 16 of the 31 cells \emph{reverses} $\denc$'s sign on 4 of
the 16 cells we ran it on (every Moirai/ETTm2 cell, 2 of which are among the
seven survivors below), and 15 cells rest on the ordinary freeze alone
(Appendix~\ref{app:strictfreeze}).

\subsection{What Fine-Tuning Does to the Seven Survivors}
\label{sec:forecasting:results}
% ============================================================

The seven cells Experiment 1 admits are the entire evidence base for a claim
about damage to valuable features, and they do not support one (Appendix
Table~\ref{tab:gate_survivors}).
% The clause naming the drift analyses' 31-cell scope is cut here, not lost: S4's "One rung of eight"
% paragraph states that the 20-cell union rather than the seven is what every drift analysis runs on,
% and S6 opens on the same scope. Three statements of one scope is two too many on a 9-page body.

\textbf{On four of the seven, full fine-tuning improves the checkpoint in every
seed.} On
Moirai-Base at $h{=}96$ and $h{=}192$ and Moirai-Large at $h{=}96$, all on ETTh2,
forgetting is $-31.4\%$, $-36.3\%$ and $-42.4\%$ against zero-shot, negative
in 3/3 seeds each, at CKA $0.460$, $0.396$ and $0.626$, the largest
representational change among the seven.  Freezing the encoder captures only part of that gain
($-9.1\%$, $-16.1\%$, $-21.6\%$), so encoder adaptation is where most of the
improvement comes from.  The fourth, Moirai-Base/ETTm2 at $h{=}192$, improves by a
smaller $8.4\%$ and is the one survivor where freezing does slightly better
($-9.7\%$), at CKA $0.488$.

\textbf{On the remaining three, the mean harm does not survive inspection.}
Moirai-Small/ETTh2 at $h{=}192$ reads forg.$_\text{B}{=}{+}1.99{\pm}3.32$ with 5 of
10 seeds positive: a coin flip on the sign, and the closest the matrix comes to
degradation.  At $h{=}96$ the positive mean ($+9.71{\pm}10.58$, 4 of 10 seeds) is
produced by one seed that diverges under the \emph{frozen} encoder too, so it is an
optimisation failure and not an encoder-drift effect; we report means including it
throughout and decompose it in Appendix~\ref{app:gatecorrection}.
Moirai-Small/ETTm2 at $h{=}192$ runs the other way (a mean $1.10\%$ improvement
with 3 of 5 seeds harmful), so its sign is a seed draw in either direction.

\subsection{Sample Size, and What Mitigation Is For}
\label{sec:forecasting:replication}
% ============================================================

Sweeping $n{\in}\{500,\ldots,10\mathrm{k}\}$ on Moirai-Small/ETTh2 at $h{=}96$
(one of the seven survivors, so a sweep where a pre-trained advantage does
exist) separates the two readings directly: drift grows monotonically with $n$
(CKA $0.949$ at 500 to $0.518$ at 10k) while forgetting is non-monotonic and
reverses sign, $+14\%$ at 500, $-2.9\%$ by 2k, $-5.3{\pm}2.1\%$ at 10k (7/10
negative). \textbf{If drift magnitude predicted harm these columns would track;
they do not.}  Whatever harm exists is confined to Small at $n{\leq}1$k, while
the largest spread across seeds sits at the $n{=}5$k crossover
(Table~\ref{tab:sample_sweep}, Figure~\ref{fig:dissociation},
Appendix~\ref{app:n5k_trajectories}).  \textbf{The $n{=}10$k sign is fragile}: an
earlier three-seed record read at its final epoch gives $+10.6\%$
against the $-5.3\%$ above, on four differences at once and so isolating no cause
(Appendix~\ref{app:limitations}).

\textbf{What mitigation is for.} Five strategies spanning unconstrained
fine-tuning to full freezing (LoRA, EWC, L2-SP, frozen encoder, strict
freeze) are reported in Appendix~\ref{app:full_tables}, on
Moirai-Small/ETTh2, the one cell where we ran the whole spectrum: weight
anchoring does not help there and LoRA does, but extending LoRA to eight
further value-cells leaves full fine-tuning ahead on four of them
(Appendix~\ref{app:loravaluecells}).

\subsection{No Robust Degradation Instance Survives a Prespecified Definition}
\label{sec:analysis:degradation}
% ============================================================

% Retitled and reframed: "no cell meets the definition" invites the reading that the zero is an
% artefact of a strict definition, so the survival framing leads and the relaxed ladder follows in the
% NEXT sentence rather than in a forward pointer. The relaxation sentence is the one that used to sit
% at the end of the paragraph below; it is moved, not added.
Applying the three-clause definition of \S\ref{sec:method} to all 31
intervention cells returns \textbf{zero cells}: \textbf{no robust,
seed-consistent degradation instance survives a definition fixed in advance}.
Relaxing that definition does not manufacture one either: across 8 rungs from the
weakest defensible one up to ours, at either gate threshold, \textbf{the largest
count anywhere is 2}, and it is reached only by the rung that drops the means
clause altogether and checks seed signs alone; every rung that keeps the means
clause admits at most 1, the 5/10 seed split named below
(Appendix~\ref{app:defsensitivity}).

Clause~(i) removes 24 of the 31 outright: they have no measured pre-trained
advantage, so a worse post-fine-tuning score is benign replacement rather than
damage.  Of the seven that survive it, four fail clause~(ii) in the strongest
possible way: full fine-tuning \emph{improves} MSE, by 8--42\% with every seed
agreeing.  That leaves three, and all three fail clause~(ii) cell by cell
(\S\ref{sec:forecasting:results}); Moirai-Small/ETTh2 at $h{=}192$ remains the
closest the matrix comes.
% Round 7. This paragraph used to give only the 20-cell aggregate, which is an average over cells that
% disagree and so cannot say which cells are decisive. The per-cell adjusted calls can, and they make a
% sharper statement against our own screen than the conjunction count could: the screen rejects both
% cells where preservation decisively wins and admits two where adaptation decisively wins. The
% aggregate is kept as the last sentence because two registered claims match its wording.
\textbf{Per-cell inference sharpens this rather than rescuing it.}  Paired
$95\%$ intervals on $\denc$, Benjamini--Hochberg adjusted across the 31 cells,
call \textbf{2 cells freezing-better, 4 adaptation-better and 25
inconclusive}, and the two where freezing decisively wins are
Moirai-Small/ETTh1 at both horizons, which the screen \emph{rejects}, while two
of the four where adaptation decisively wins \emph{pass} it, at $+0.925$ and
$+0.875$.  The interval form of the definition returns zero
cells, as the registered form does.  \textbf{What the inconclusive calls cost is
itself reportable}: the survivors' own dispersion gives minimum detectable
effects of $4.7$--$14.7$~pp, and Moirai-Small/ETTh2 $h{=}96$ and
Moirai-Base/ETTm2 $h{=}192$ would need 182 and 212 paired seeds, so we report
those two as \emph{unresolvable under this design} rather than as absent.
Aggregated over the 20 value-cells with clustered resampling, $\denc{=}{+}0.7$
with CI $[-11.1,{+}14.1]$ and 10 of 20 positive
(Appendix~\ref{app:pairedinference}).
Changing the gate's \emph{estimator} does admit cells: six of the ladder's eight
rungs admit none, persistence admits three and GBM one.  All four rest on a
denominator within $5\%$ of the training-mean floor and roughly twice the fitted
ridge's error on the same windows, so a \emph{weaker} baseline is what manufactures
a degradation cell (Appendix~\ref{app:baselines}).

\textbf{What we are and are not saying.}  Not that fine-tuning never damages a
pre-trained capability, but that across 32 screened cells we could not construct
one instance that survives its own definition.  These benchmarks are the wrong
place to look: on no cell does the advantage survive every admissible rung of an
eight-baseline ladder, and on the seven that clear our ridge gate, fine-tuning on
500--1{,}000 samples usually improves on it (\S\ref{sec:limitations},
Appendix~\ref{app:corrections}).
