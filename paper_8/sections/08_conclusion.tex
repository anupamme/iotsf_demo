\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
% ============================================================

The primary contribution is a reusable diagnostic toolkit (value-gate
screening and controlled encoder comparisons) whose design transfers to any
backbone. The dissociation between drift magnitude and task outcome is
validated on Moirai, with Chronos/M4 confirming the value-gate
cross-backbone. A probe-based second axis reported earlier is retracted
(\S\ref{sec:retraction}); no conclusion here depends on it.
\textbf{Practitioner heuristic} (cells tested; not yet universal): compute ZS
vs.\ Linear; gate-fail $\Rightarrow$ skip preservation; moderate gate-pass
$\Rightarrow$ monitor CKA; strong gate-pass $\Rightarrow$ try frozen encoder first.
