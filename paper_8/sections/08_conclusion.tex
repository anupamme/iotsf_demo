\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
% ============================================================

\textbf{Practitioner summary.}
Use LoRA (not full fine-tuning) as default for Moirai adaptation.
On Moirai-Small with scarce data, LoRA preserves representations
(CKA$\approx$0.98); on Moirai-Large, reduce LR by $10{\times}$ to
$10^{-5}$ (cross-dataset validated on ETTh1/ETTh2/ETTm2).
If adaptation is unnecessary, freeze the encoder.
The value-gate heuristic (ZS advantage $>$20\% vs.\ Linear) screens
for settings where pre-trained features are worth preserving.

\textbf{Main finding: a cost--benefit characterisation.}
Fine-tuning imposes geometric cost (CKA drops from 0.949 at $n{=}500$
to 0.518 at $n{=}10$k) with task-aligned functional benefit
($\Delta R^2{>}0$ in 10/10 seeds on ETTh2/ETTm2 at $n{=}10$k;
Ridge $\Delta R^2$ rises to $+$0.67$\pm$0.23).
Task forgetting is protocol-conditional: non-monotonic in $n$,
$-$5.3$\pm$6.2\% under early-stopping (7/10 negative) vs.\
$+$7.5$\pm$8.2\% final-epoch at $n{=}10$k.
The $\Delta R^2{>}0$ majority is more robust than forgetting sign.
ETTh1 at $n{=}10$k shows task-improvement-without-decodability-gain
(10/10 negative forgetting, 2/10 positive $\Delta R^2$), narrowing
the invariant to ETTh2/ETTm2.

\textbf{Contribution tiering.}
Backbone-agnostic methodological contributions (value-gate framework,
LoRA-Large 10$\times$ LR recipe, controlled-comparison design, uni2ts
patch) are robust.
Empirical findings (three-regime taxonomy, per-seed $\Delta R^2{>}0$
invariant) are Moirai-ETT specific and require replication for
universality.
Nine-of-nine gate failures (Chronos/TimesFM/MOMENT on ETT) are a
substantive negative result about ETT coverage, independent of
Moirai-specific dissociation generalization.

\textbf{Limitations} (Appendix~\ref{app:limitations}): dissociation
established on Moirai only; absolute $R^2(\text{FT})$ remains negative
across all $n$ (deep-floor regime); gate-passing non-Moirai backbone
on Traffic or Exchange datasets is highest-priority future work for
broadening single-backbone scope.
