\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
% ============================================================

\textbf{Methodology.}
The primary contribution is a reusable diagnostic toolkit for
time-series foundation model fine-tuning: value-gate screening, three
controlled comparisons (frozen-encoder, random-init, layer-wise
unfreeze), task-orthogonal probe protocol, LoRA-Large LR recipe, and
CUDA-deterministic per-seed reporting.
These transfer to any backbone; applying the value-gate before
investing in drift diagnosis prevents wasted effort on settings where
pre-trained features have no measurable value.

\textbf{Case-study summary (Moirai-ETT).}
Fine-tuning imposes geometric cost (CKA $0.949{\to}0.518$) with
protocol-dependent task outcome: harmful at $n{\leq}1$k, improving
in 7/10 seeds at $n{=}10$k under early stopping (Wilson CI [0.40,\,0.89]).
The task-orthogonal probe asymmetry ($\Delta R^2{<}0$ on positive-floor
probes, $\Delta R^2{>}0$ on the trained head) is cross-cell and
operationalises task-specific encoder restructuring.
This case study illustrates the methodology; replication on additional
gate-passing backbone$\times$dataset cells is required before claiming
universality.

\textbf{Limitations} (Appendix~\ref{app:limitations}): case study
established on Moirai only; absolute $R^2(\text{FT})$ remains negative
(deep-floor regime; positive-floor probes show $\Delta R^2{<}0$,
constraining but not eliminating the regime confound);
gate-passing non-Moirai backbone on Traffic or Exchange is
highest-priority future work.
