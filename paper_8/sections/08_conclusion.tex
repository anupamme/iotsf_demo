\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
% ============================================================

Fine-tuning overwrites pre-trained features (10--16\% MSE on ETT at
$n{=}500$, resolving by $n{=}2$k; CKA 0.31--0.45 on IoT yet performance
improves); consequences depend on pre-training value and data. LoRA
on Moirai-Small is the best trade-off (CKA$\approx$0.98) but fails on
Large ($+$25.7$\pm$3.2\% forg., rank escalation stays $+$19 to $+$30\%)
and on IoT. Guidance: Small$+$scarce$\to$LoRA; Large or marginal$\to$
freezing or NLL$+$early stopping; 200-window$\to$from-scratch CNN-NLL.
Limitations (App.~\ref{app:limitations}): dissociation on Moirai
only (Chronos 0.304, TimesFM 0.242 gate-fail); Traffic/ILI/2nd-backbone open.
