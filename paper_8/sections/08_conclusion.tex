\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
% ============================================================

\textbf{Methodology.}
The primary contribution is a reusable diagnostic toolkit---value-gate
screening, controlled comparisons, and task-orthogonal probes---that
transfers to any backbone.

\textbf{Findings.}
Probe asymmetry replicates across datasets (ETTh2, ETTm2, ILI: all
10/10) and sizes (Small 10/10, Base 9/10); strong gate-pass predicts
encoder stability (Chronos-M4, CKA${\geq}$0.95).
Limitations (Appendix~\ref{app:limitations}): probe asymmetry on Moirai
only; absolute $R^2(\text{FT})$ remains negative.

\textbf{Practitioner protocol.}
(1)~Compute ZS MSE vs.\ lookback-matched Linear;
(2)~gate-pass${<}$20\%: fine-tune freely;
(3)~gate-pass 20--80\%: expect restructuring---use LoRA with
$10{\times}$ LR reduction, monitor CKA;
(4)~gate-pass${>}$80\%: encoder near-optimal---use frozen-encoder.
