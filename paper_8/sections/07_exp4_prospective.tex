\subsection{A Pre-Registered Prospective Test at Zero Prevalence}
\label{sec:analysis:prospective}
% Four paragraphs until 21 Sep 2026, and their length was itself making a claim the arm cannot
% support: a reviewer read the space they occupied as a refutation of the gate. The full record --
% both registered rules, the eight cells, the threshold sweep and the corrected-gate check -- moved
% verbatim to Appendix \ref{app:prospective2}, which is scanned by scripts/check_paper_numbers.py, so
% every registered number still has its sentence. What is left here is the arm's one result and the
% bound its prevalence puts on it.
Everything above is retrospective, and a diagnostic that only explains cells it
has seen is worth little, so we tested ours forward: predictions for eight unrun
cells were committed to git before any outcome run for them existed, and the
scoring script decides nothing (Appendix~\ref{app:prospective2}).
\textbf{The outcome the gate was registered to predict never occurs.}  None of
the eight cells degraded; the gate flagged all eight, giving \textbf{TP~0, FP~8,
FN~0}: precision $0.00$, while \textbf{sensitivity and specificity are both
unestimable}.  \textbf{That makes this a test at zero prevalence, and the
prevalence bounds what it can say}: it establishes that the gate's positive
predictions are wrong here---and not merely at the $0.20$ operating point, since
re-scoring the same predictor at every threshold we swept gives precision $0.00$
at all of them (Appendix~\ref{app:prospective2})---and it establishes nothing
about the gate's \emph{sensitivity}, which an
empty positive class cannot estimate.  It is not a demonstration that the gate
cannot work; it is a demonstration that these benchmarks supply nothing for it
to work on.

\textbf{What survives.} Not the gate as a predictor of harm, nor as a screen for
when preservation is worth the effort---here it fired eight times and was wrong
eight times---but the gate as a \emph{disqualifier}: a cell that fails it cannot
supply evidence about damage to pre-trained features, and 24 of our 31 fail it.

\subsection{A Pre-Registered Positive Control: We Could Not Construct Forgetting}
\label{sec:exp4:poscontrol}
% The reviewer-critical addition. Everything above establishes that these benchmarks contain no
% degradation cell; the obvious objection is that a diagnostic cannot be shown to fail at detection
% when there is nothing to detect. This subsection answers it with an engineered regime, and it is
% written to be readable as a NULL: the pre-registration fixed all three outcomes in advance, and the
% one that fired is the one that says the control failed to construct. The full ladder, both
% checkpoints and the divergence are in Appendix \ref{app:poscontrol}.
A null about a detector is only as good as the
regime it was measured in: \textbf{a diagnostic cannot be shown to fail at
ordering damage if there is no damage to order}.  So we tried to build the
damage.  Task A is the strongest capability the screen admits
(Moirai-Small/ETTh2, $h{=}192$, $\Vb{\text{ridge}}{=}{+}0.932$); task B is a
synthetic series, fixed by hash before any run, whose correct forecast is the
\emph{negation} of the seasonal-naive prior that is task A's own strongest
admissible rung.  We fine-tuned on B and measured A over the registered
$\text{lr}\in\{10^{-4},10^{-3},10^{-2}\}$ crossed with both conditions at 3 seeds,
with all three outcomes and the destruction threshold committed before the first
run (Appendix~\ref{app:poscontrol}).

\textbf{The pre-registered outcome that fired is the third: destruction was not
achieved.}  Task B was learned in \textbf{21 of 21} runs (minimum $+56.6\%$
against a registered $20\%$), so this is genuine conflicting learning and not
weight thrashing.  The registered rule was
retention$_A\!\geq\!50\%$ in a majority of seeds under full fine-tuning; the
maximum over all 21 runs was $+26.7\%$, in one seed, against an evaluator noise
floor of $0.48$~pp.

\textbf{The attempt nonetheless buys a sharper dissociation than the benchmark
cells can supply.}  At that top rung the representation is driven almost to
orthogonality---CKA on task A's inputs $0.063$, $\ell_2$ weight drift $2795$,
against $0.999$ and $7.2$ at the gentlest rung---and task A gets \emph{better},
by $23.3\pm2.0\%$ in every seed; across all 21 runs the correlation between CKA
and retention is $\rho{=}{+}0.466$, the wrong sign.  \textbf{A representation can
be moved most of the way to orthogonality by training measurably in conflict with
a capability, and the capability survives.}
