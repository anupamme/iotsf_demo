\label{sec:analysis}

\textbf{Drift is consistent; consequences depend on pre-training value.}
Table~\ref{tab:cross_domain} spans seven settings: on ETT
(28--45\% ZS advantage) drift causes forgetting (10--16\% MSE,
CKA 0.81--0.94); on IoT (marginal ZS) CKA drops further (0.31--0.45)
yet performance \emph{improves}; on Weather the ZS gate fails
(0.41 vs 0.22), delimiting the claim.

\textbf{Model-size replication.}
Moirai-Base (91M) is a boundary case: unfrozen-Base drifts
(CKA$=$0.460) and improves $-$44.1\%, while frozen-Base improves
further to $-$47.4\% at CKA$=$0.997 --- drift and forgetting are
\emph{dissociable}, not drift is always beneficial; Base's gain is
capacity and freezing retains it without paying the drift cost.
Moirai-Large (311M) at $n{=}500$: CKA$=$0.626$\pm$0.165,
$+$10.5$\pm$9.5\% forg.\ (3 seeds); frozen-Large $+$3.2$\pm$0.6\%;
LoRA $r{=}8$ fails ($+$25.7$\pm$3.2\%, CKA 0.973, 3 seeds) and
rank escalation does not rescue it ($r{\in}\{16,32,64\}$: $+$22.5,
$+$19.0, $+$29.9\%, seed 42; Appendix~\ref{app:lora_rank}) --- the
Small-recipe does not transfer to Large at any rank we tested.
A from-scratch CNN-NLL control (AUC$=$0.598$\pm$0.041) outperforms
unfrozen Moirai at IoT's 200-window scale (Appendix~\ref{app:cnn_detail}).

\textbf{CKA and linear probing are dissociable.}
Ridge probing on frozen encoder reps (Table~\ref{tab:sample_sweep})
shows $\Delta R^2$ rising with $n$ ($+$0.12 at 500 $\to +$0.68 at
10{,}000) while CKA drops (0.949 $\to$ 0.429); MLP probe replicates
($+$0.73$\pm$.75 at $n{=}2$k, 3 seeds; $+$1.54$\pm$.24 at $n{=}10$k,
2 seeds).  At $n{=}10{,}000$ representations have drifted substantially
yet are \emph{functionally superior} with near-zero forgetting ---
directly dissociating geometric drift from information loss
(random-init CKA$<$0.001; Appendix~\ref{app:cka_calibration}).

\textbf{$n{=}5{,}000$ bimodality.}
The 7-seed $+$6.6$\pm$10.0\% variance splits into low-forgetting
(42, 101, 123, 202: $-$0.1$\pm$3.7\%) and high-forgetting
(303, 456, 789: $+$15.5$\pm$7.3\%) modes. Per-epoch trajectories
(App.~\ref{app:n5k_trajectories}, Fig.~\ref{fig:n5k_trajectories})
separate them by val-MSE epoch-of-minimum ($6.5\pm2.1$ vs.\
$0.7\pm0.5$): high-forg.\ seeds overshoot in epoch 1, motivating
early-stopping. LR/2 ablation: bimodality persists ($+$5.8$\pm$15.7\%,
7 seeds) --- not a pure LR artefact.

An IoT logistic-regression probe also replicates the dissociation
direction ($\Delta$AUC$=+$0.013$\pm$0.006, CKA$=$0.370, 3 seeds);
limitations and practitioner guidance: \S\ref{sec:conclusion},
Appendix~\ref{app:limitations}.
