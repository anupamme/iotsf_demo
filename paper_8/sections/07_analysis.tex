\section{Cross-Domain Analysis and Discussion}
\label{sec:analysis}
% ============================================================

\textbf{Task forgetting is protocol-dependent.}
Forgetting is non-monotonic in $n$ ($+$14\% at 500, $-$2.9\% at 2k,
$-$5.3$\pm$6.2\% at 10k ES, 7/10 negative) with protocol-dependent sign
(final-epoch $+$7.5$\pm$8.2\%): of 10 CUDA seeds at $n{=}10$k, 7 converge
monotonically (best epochs 3--5) and 3 overshoot late (epochs 12--18),
the source of the sign difference (Appendix~\ref{app:n5k_trajectories}).
We therefore report the sign of forgetting at this cell as protocol-dependent
rather than settled, and do not lead on it.

\textbf{Second cell: ETTm2 $n{=}10$k drifts with the \emph{opposite} task outcome.}
ETTm2 ($h{=}96$, $n{=}10$k, CUDA $k{=}10$) drifts substantially yet
forgetting is $-29.2{\pm}2.1\%$---a large improvement---in a cell that
\emph{fails} the gate. Drift there overwrites features that were not
outperforming Linear, so the same magnitude of representational change is
benign. Together with ETTh2, this is the dissociation in its clearest form:
comparable drift, opposite consequence, and the gate---not the drift
reading---is what separates them.

\textbf{ETTh1 $n{=}10$k: boundary case.}
On ETTh1 ($h{=}96$, $n{=}10$k, $k{=}10$), forgetting resolves
($-6.1{\pm}3.6$\%) while CKA falls to $0.727$: another cell where
substantial drift accompanies improvement rather than harm.

\textbf{Capacity sensitivity: Moirai-Base as second gate-passing cell.}
Moirai-Base (91M) gate-passes ETTh2 ($R^2_{\text{task}}(\text{PT}){=}{+}0.31$)
but behaves qualitatively differently from Small: task \emph{improvement} at
both $n$ (forg.\ $-56.9$\% at 500, $-54.2$\% at 10k; 10/10 negative) despite
\emph{aggressive} drift (CKA $0.25$--$0.33$): the most heavily drifted cell
in the study is also one that improves.
Base is protocol-robust and improves even at low $n$ where Small degrades.
Moirai-Large (311M) mirrors Small at $n{=}500$ ($+$10.5\%); LoRA-Large is
rescued by $10\times$ LR reduction (Appendix~\ref{app:lora_rank}).
\textbf{Step-size falsification:} Base at $10{\times}$ LR restructures more
aggressively than Small at default LR (CKA$=$0.135 vs.\ 0.937) yet
\emph{still improves} ($-58.2$\%, 10/10 positive), while Small at
$0.1{\times}$ LR merely near-freezes (CKA$=$0.987). Thus
capacity is an independent buffer against destructive drift, not merely a
lower effective step size (Appendix~\ref{app:step_size}).

\textbf{IoT negative control.}
Moirai ZS AUC$=$0.481 (sub-random); drift (CKA 0.31--0.45) is benign
because from-scratch CNN matches fine-tuned Moirai; the value-gate
correctly screens out this cell (Appendix~\ref{app:cnn_detail}).

% ============================================================
\subsection{Non-Moirai Backbones: All Nine Cells Gate-Fail}
\label{sec:forecasting:nonmoirai}
% ============================================================

Matched-protocol ZS across nine non-Moirai backbone${\times}$ETT cells
(Chronos, TimesFM-2.5, MOMENT $\times$ ETT) all gate-fail ($-$14\% to
$-$148\% vs.\ Linear; Appendix~\ref{app:limitations}).
Corpus composition is the most parsimonious account:
\textbf{Chronos-T5-Small gate-passes on M4-Monthly} (84.5\%, in its training
corpus) while gate-failing on ETT ($-$43\%)---same architecture, opposite
gate outcome; and Moirai-Small itself gate-fails on Traffic/Exchange/
Electricity, so \emph{pre-training emphasis} rather than mere corpus
inclusion determines gate-pass.
On M4-Monthly, full diagnostics (10 seeds, conditions B/D) give
CKA${\approx}$0.98--0.999 and no distinguishable forgetting
($+0.5$\% at $n{=}10$k): strong gate-pass coincides with encoder stability,
consistent with the value-gate as a regime indicator
(Appendix~\ref{app:chronos_detail}).

\textbf{ILI: third gate-passing cell (10 seeds).}
\label{sec:analysis:ili}
Moirai-Small on ILI (weekly influenza, $h{=}24$, gate-pass${=}$57\%,
conditions B/D) yields CKA$=$0.478${\pm}$0.022 (restructuring) with large
task improvement (forgetting$={-}80.7$\%, 10/10 negative).
\textbf{Correction.} We previously reported that the frozen-encoder control
(D) gives \emph{zero} improvement in all 10 seeds, and concluded that the
encoder is the sole locus of adaptation (B$-$D${=}{-}80.7$~pp). That is
withdrawn. The script short-circuited condition~D: it assigned the zero-shot
MSE as the fine-tuned MSE and wrote a forgetting of exactly $0.0$, so no
frozen-encoder model was ever trained and the ``$0.0\%$ in all 10 seeds''
was a single constant, not a measurement. Re-running condition~D so that it
trains --- encoder frozen, the remaining parameters updated, as in the Moirai
cells of \S\ref{sec:forecasting} --- gives B$-$D${=}{-}62.6{\pm}4.6$ (10/10
seeds), not $-80.7$: the frozen encoder recovers a large share of the gain
rather than none of it. Its CKA is $0.76$--$0.90$, not the asserted $1.000$,
because the projections feeding the encoder remain trainable. Condition~B is
unaffected and reproduces ($-80.0{\pm}2.0$ against the published
$-80.7{\pm}0.9$; CKA $0.456$ against $0.478$), so the entire discrepancy was
the untrained control. Per-seed values accompany the released code.
The pre-registered CKA-intermediate prediction (0.90--0.95) is
\textbf{falsified}---actual CKA is 0.478. This revises the spectrum
hypothesis: restructuring extends from 28\% through 57\% gate-pass, with
stability beginning above ${\approx}$80\% (Chronos/M4).
