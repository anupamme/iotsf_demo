\section{Limitations and Broader Impact}
\label{sec:limitations}
% ============================================================

Full detail is in Appendix~\ref{app:limitations}.
(1)~\emph{A null result is not a proof of absence.} We report that no cell in
our matrix meets the degradation definition, not that degradation does not
occur.  What would settle it is a cell with a \emph{large} pre-trained margin
over a fitted baseline, and that is the combination our benchmarks do not
supply: on 27 of 32 cells the margin is negative, and on the five that pass
the largest is $R^2_\text{task}(\text{PT}){=}{+}0.464$.
(2)~\emph{The screen may be too weak in the other direction.} A lookback-96
ridge map is a low bar, and a cell that fails it may still hold capability a
stronger baseline would not capture.
(3)~\emph{Condition~D is a control on encoder weight updates, not on
representations.} It leaves the input projection and mask encoding trainable,
so its CKA is $0.969$--$0.9999$ on Moirai and as low as $0.38$ on TimesFM.
Condition~H (strict freeze, CKA exactly $1.0000$) closes that gap on 12 of the
31 cells, including all five survivors, whose readings it confirms; the other
19 rest on condition~D alone (Appendix~\ref{app:strictfreeze}).
(4)~\emph{Seed counts are small.} Cells carry 3 to 10 seeds, and the unanimity
requirement in clauses~(ii) and~(iii) is doing real work at those counts: the
closest candidate is excluded on a 5/10 split.
(5)~\emph{Horizon confounds the cross-backbone comparison.} The non-Moirai arm
runs at $h{=}24$ and the Moirai arm at $h{\in}\{96,192\}$, on different
normalisations; we report them separately and never pool them.
(6)~\emph{A retracted axis.} The linear-probe $\Delta R^2$ diagnostic is
withdrawn (Appendix~\ref{sec:retraction}), so we offer no account of \emph{what} the
encoder restructured---only whether the restructuring was necessary, which the
frozen-encoder comparison answers.
(7)~\emph{Similarity is not mechanism.} A representation can drift far and still
carry the task information in a transformed basis, and a small drift can destroy
one critical direction; our negative claim is about \emph{identifying} damage
from similarity alone, not about CKA's validity as a measure of similarity.
(8)~\emph{Also unsettled:} the forgetting sign at $n{=}10$k is
protocol-dependent; the ILI cell is small (${\approx}450$ windows); and two
pre-registered predictions failed (\S\ref{sec:analysis:prospective},
\S\ref{sec:analysis:ili}).

\textbf{Broader impact.} A diagnostic study on public forecasting benchmarks.
The intended effect is to
raise the bar for claims about forgetting in time-series foundation models: a
paper reporting that fine-tuning damaged pre-trained features should say what
baseline established that the features were valuable, and how that baseline
was fit.  The risk of a null result is that it is over-read as licence to
fine-tune carelessly; \S\ref{sec:analysis:degradation} says only that the harm
cannot be demonstrated on these benchmarks, and the controlled comparison
remains the way to answer the question for any particular cell.
