% reviewer reported having to work out which one that was. What survives here is the spine sentence, the

\begin{abstract}
Fine-tuning a time-series foundation model reshapes its internal
representations, and that reshaping is routinely read as catastrophic
forgetting to be prevented.  Preventing it presupposes something worth
preserving.  \textbf{CKA tells us that the representation changed; a
fine-tune-versus-frozen-encoder intervention tells us whether that change was
valuable.  Across the cells we evaluate, the former does not reliably predict
the latter.}
Screening \textbf{32 cells}---three backbones (Moirai-S/B/L, Chronos-T5,
TimesFM-2.5), six datasets, two horizons---against a lookback-96 linear
regression \emph{fit on the target data} and scored on windows disjoint from
every outcome we report, only \textbf{7 of the 31} scored cells show the
pre-trained model beating that baseline, and all seven are Moirai on two of the
six datasets.  On those seven, full fine-tuning \emph{improves} MSE in five.
\textbf{No cell meets a degradation definition we fixed in advance}, under any
fine-tuning protocol we evaluated.
The outcome nonetheless spans $-51.8\%$ to $+46.1\%$ against zero-shot, and CKA
\textbf{does not provide a reliable ordering} of it: within Moirai, the only
backbone with enough cells to ask, $\rho{=}{+}0.168$ (clustered CI
$[-0.469,{+}0.717]$), and no cut on CKA separates the cells encoder adaptation
helped from the cells it hurt.  Under an analysis fixed before it was run, CKA adds
\textbf{no} out-of-sample predictive information beyond backbone identity: across
15 leave-one-cluster-out folds it \emph{lowers} $R^2$ from $+0.201$ to $+0.176$.
Two measurement choices decide this: the frozen-encoder comparison must be scored
on \textbf{held-out} windows---4 of 31 cells otherwise reverse sign, every one of
them flattering the frozen encoder, and two of the four are cells that pass the
screen---and the baseline must be \textbf{fitted},
since an unfitted per-window trend extrapolation that a constant predictor beats
puts 16 of 21 Moirai cells on the wrong side of the screen.
\end{abstract}
