\begin{abstract}
We present a case study of Moirai fine-tuning~\cite{woo2024moirai} on ETT
forecasting benchmarks, documenting a \textbf{cost--benefit gap}: fine-tuning
imposes geometric cost (CKA drops from 0.949 at $n{=}500$ to $\approx$0.52 at
$n{=}10{,}000$) while producing a task-aligned functional benefit
(Ridge $\Delta R^2{>}0$ on the trained 96-step head in 10/10 seeds on
ETTh2 and ETTm2; $\Delta R^2{\leq}0$ on untrained heads).
Task forgetting is a protocol-dependent consequence: $-$5.3$\pm$6.2\%
under early-stopped checkpointing (7/10 negative), $+$7.5$\pm$8.2\% under
final-epoch (dominated by late-epoch overshoot in 3 seeds).
We distinguish three sample-size regimes as a descriptive taxonomy:
low-$n$ forgetting, high-$n$ improvement (ETTh2/ETTm2), and a boundary
case (ETTh1: task resolves but $\Delta R^2$ is 2/10 positive).
Three controls rule out confounds: a frozen-encoder control
($\Delta R^2{=}{-}0.048{\pm}0.008$, 14$\times$ smaller than fine-tuned)
rules out data-distribution effects; a random-init control
(5 seeds, $\Delta R^2{=}{-}0.199{\pm}0.205$, 4/5 negative) rules out
``any encoder fine-tuned on this objective''; a layer-wise unfreeze
(top 3 of 6 layers sufficient for $\Delta R^2{>}0$, 3/3 seeds) localises
the effect.

\textbf{Contribution tiering.}
We distinguish methodological contributions (backbone-agnostic: a value-gate
framework for pre-training quality, LoRA-Large 10$\times$ LR rescue recipe
cross-dataset validated on ETTh1/ETTh2/ETTm2, controlled-comparison designs,
and a uni2ts PackedStdScaler patch) from empirical findings
(Moirai-ETT specific: the three-regime taxonomy, per-seed $\Delta R^2{>}0$
invariant on ETTh2/ETTm2).
Nine non-Moirai backbones (Chronos-T5-Small/Base/Large, TimesFM, MOMENT-base)
gate-fail on ETT (ZS $<$20\% over Linear), a finding about ETT coverage in
their pre-training corpora independent of the Moirai-specific results.
\textbf{Practitioner takeaway:} Use LoRA as default for Moirai adaptation;
on Moirai-Large, reduce LR by 10$\times$ before escalating rank.
\end{abstract}
